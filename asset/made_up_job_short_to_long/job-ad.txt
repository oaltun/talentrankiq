Senior Software Engineer (Backend & Platform)
Northwind Cloud, Inc. | Remote (US/Canada) | Full-time

About the role
Northwind Cloud is hiring a Senior Software Engineer to help design, build, and operate the
distributed systems that power our billing and identity platform. You will join a team of twelve
engineers who own services end to end, from architecture and implementation through deployment,
observability, and on-call. This is a hands-on individual contributor role with significant
technical leadership and mentorship expectations.

What you will do
- Design and implement backend services in Go or Rust that handle high-throughput, low-latency traffic.
- Build and operate event-driven pipelines using Apache Kafka, including exactly-once processing and replay tooling.
- Own services deployed on Kubernetes, including Helm charts, rolling deployments, and autoscaling.
- Model and optimize relational data in PostgreSQL; write migrations and tune slow queries.
- Define and maintain gRPC and REST APIs using Protobuf and OpenAPI.
- Instrument services with OpenTelemetry, Prometheus, and Grafana; participate in a humane on-call rotation.
- Write unit, integration, and contract tests; drive test coverage and reliability improvements.
- Review code, mentor mid-level engineers, and contribute to architecture decision records.
- Collaborate with product managers and designers to scope and deliver customer-facing capabilities.

Required qualifications
- 5+ years of professional backend engineering experience.
- Strong proficiency in at least one of Go, Rust, or Java, with production experience.
- Deep knowledge of distributed systems fundamentals: consensus, retries, idempotency, caching, and backpressure.
- Hands-on experience with PostgreSQL or a comparable relational database at scale.
- Experience deploying and operating services on Kubernetes in production.
- Solid understanding of observability, incident response, and root-cause analysis.
- Excellent written communication and a track record of mentoring other engineers.

Nice to have
- Apache Kafka or comparable streaming infrastructure in production.
- gRPC and Protobuf API design.
- Terraform or other infrastructure-as-code tooling.
- Experience in a regulated industry such as fintech or healthcare.
- Contributions to open-source projects.
